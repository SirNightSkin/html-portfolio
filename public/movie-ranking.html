Must See On The Big Screen

Jodeci's List of Favorite Movies

2025

Captain America: A Brave New World

This movie in my opinion has the most substance compared to any marvel movie to date. As far as character development, plot line, story, and visual story telling.

Mufasa: Live Action

I was skeptical but this movie was cliche in the most emotional way. Also the songs were so catchy and beautiful. I mean I always wanted a Brother.

2024

Wicked

Do I even need to explain why this is one of the top movies of 2024!? Visually stunning with complex scenes and cinematography. The heart wrenching character development of Elphaba. I guess I am a musical fan, because these songs were so powerful.

MOANA 2

I'm not crying you are! Such a heart warming, suspensful rollercoaster of a movie. Unique, visually stunning, and the cultural representation. Muah Chef's kiss.
